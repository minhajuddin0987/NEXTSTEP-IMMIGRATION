Italy Student Visa — Step by Step

Everything you need to get your Italian university offer letter and then submit your student visa at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka.

🎓
Step 01 — University Application
Getting Your Offer Letter
  • ✓
    Academic Transcripts & Certificates — SSC, HSC, Bachelor's with certified English or Italian translations
  • ✓
    Dichiarazione di Valore (DoV) — Official declaration of value of your Bangladeshi degree, issued by the Italian Embassy in Dhaka
  • ✓
    Motivational / Cover Letter — 500–800 words explaining your reasons for choosing this specific program and Italy
  • ✓
    Language Certificate — IELTS minimum 5.5–6.0 for English-taught programs; B2 Italian (CILS/PLIDA) for Italian-taught programs
  • ✓
    CV / Europass Resume — Detailing your academic background and work experience
  • ✓
    Letters of Recommendation — 2 strong references from professors or employers
  • ✓
    Valid Passport — At least 18 months validity remaining
  • ✓
    University Entrance Test (if required) — Medicine and architecture require IMAT or TOLC test
  • ✓
    Universitaly Pre-Enrollment — Register at universitaly.it before applying to most universities
🏛️
Step 02 — Embassy Submission
Visa Application Documents
  • ✓
    Type D National Long-Stay Visa Form — Completed, signed and dated student visa application
  • ✓
    University Acceptance / Enrollment Letter — Official admission confirmation from your Italian university
  • ✓
    Universitaly Pre-Enrollment Receipt — Printed confirmation from the Italian government portal
  • ✓
    Valid Passport — Original + 2 clear photocopies; at least 6 months validity
  • ✓
    Biometric Passport Photos — 2 recent passport-sized photographs on white background
  • ✓
    Financial Proof — Minimum ~€6,000–6,500/year; 6 months bank statements or scholarship letter
  • ✓
    Accommodation Proof — University dormitory booking, signed rental agreement, or host declaration
  • ✓
    Dichiarazione di Valore (DoV) — Issued by the Italian Embassy in Bangladesh — absolutely essential
  • ✓
    Health Insurance — Comprehensive coverage for Italy, minimum 12 months duration
  • ✓
    Clean Criminal Record — Issued by Bangladesh Police, attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs
  • ✓
    Medical Certificate — Recent certificate from a certified physician
  • ✓
    Visa Fee — Approximately €50 (non-refundable Italian student visa fee)

Living in Italy — The Complete Student Guide

Everything a Bangladeshi student needs to know before and after arriving in Italy — from getting your Codice Fiscale to finding accommodation, working part-time, and building your future in Europe.

💶Monthly Budget
Cost of Living in Italy
€
Total estimate: €700–€1,000/month (€1,200–1,500 in Milan or Rome)
🏠
Rent (shared room): €300–€550/month; cheaper in Padua, Naples
🛒
Groceries: €150–200/month; local markets are excellent and cheap
🚌
Transport (student): €20–35/month for city buses and metro
🍕
University canteen (mensa): Full meals for only €4–7 — heavily subsidized
💡 DSU grants can cover tuition + provide monthly stipends (€150–300) for low-income international students!
🪪First Priority
Codice Fiscale — Your Essential ID
⭐
What it is: Italy's tax code — absolutely required for everything in Italy
📋
You need it for: Bank account, renting, SIM card, university registration, healthcare
🏛️
How to get it: Italian Embassy in Dhaka before departure, or Agenzia delle Entrate in Italy
💰
Cost: Completely free — takes 15–30 minutes
💡 NEXTSTEP can guide you in obtaining your Codice Fiscale from the Italian Embassy in Dhaka.
🩺Health & Insurance
Healthcare — SSN & Insurance
🏥
SSN registration: ~€150/year for international students — full Italian public healthcare
👨‍⚕️
With SSN: free GP access, hospitals, specialists, subsidized medicine
🔒
Private insurance: Required for visa (€120–300/year)
💊
Prescriptions: €2–4 co-payment with SSN
💡 Register with SSN within your first month — it is affordable and provides real protection.
📄Residence Permit
Permesso di Soggiorno
⚡
Apply within 8 days of arrival — mandatory for all non-EU students staying 90+ days
📮
How to apply: Get the "kit" from any Post Office (Sportello Amico) — fee ~€70–100
⏳
Processing time: 3–6 months; you receive a ricevuta immediately as temporary proof
🔄
Must be renewed each academic year — bring enrollment confirmation
💡 Never miss the 8-day deadline — overstaying without applying is a serious legal issue.
💼Work Rights
Working Part-Time in Italy
⏱
20 hours/week allowed; full-time during academic breaks
💶
Typical pay: €8–12/hour in restaurants, retail, and tutoring
🇮🇹
Italian language is key: Even basic A2–B1 dramatically improves job opportunities
🌍
Find jobs on Indeed.it, InfoJobs.it and university career centers
💡 Tourism sector (summer) offers full-time hours — great income during academic break.
🚀After Graduation
Post-Study Opportunities
📆
Job-search permit: 12-month permit after degree to stay and find employment
🏭
Strong demand for graduates in engineering, IT, fashion & design, medicine
🇪🇺
An Italian degree is EU-recognized — opens doors across all 27 EU countries
🌱
Italy Startup Visa for innovative entrepreneurs available
💡 NEXTSTEP provides guidance for post-graduation residence permit applications too.
📌
Critical reminders for Italy: Apply for your Permesso di Soggiorno within 8 days of arrival. Get your Codice Fiscale as early as possible — from the Italian Embassy in Dhaka before you depart. The Dichiarazione di Valore (DoV) is essential for your degree recognition — NEXTSTEP Immigration assists you with this process. Do not leave Italy without renewing your residence permit before it expires.
